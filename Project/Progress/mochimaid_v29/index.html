Need help?

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to common booking and service questions.

Can I cancel my booking after I have already paid?

Yes. You can cancel from your booking details. Whether the full amount is refunded will depend on how close the cancellation is to the scheduled cleaning time. The final cancellation terms will always be shown before you confirm payment.

What should I do if I notice something missing after the cleaning?

Report the issue to MochiMaid support as soon as possible. The booking information, cleaner details, and service record can then be reviewed so the issue can be handled through the platform rather than privately between the customer and cleaner.

Can I add extra cleaning tasks after the cleaner has already arrived?

Yes, as long as the cleaner agrees and has enough time. Any additional task and extra charge should be added and confirmed through MochiMaid before the cleaner begins the extra work, so both sides can see the updated booking total.

What happens if my cleaner does not arrive? Will I get a refund?

If the cleaner does not arrive and the service is not provided, you can report the booking as a no-show. The booking can then be rescheduled, matched with another cleaner, or refunded according to the no-show policy.
