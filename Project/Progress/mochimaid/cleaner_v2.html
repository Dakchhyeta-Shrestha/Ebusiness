FAQ

Questions cleaners may have

What bonuses can I earn?

Bonuses reward cleaners who complete jobs regularly and keep high ratings. Amounts and criteria are not finalised for this project.

Why do I need to verify after 10 jobs?

Customers trust verified cleaners, and verification unlocks more services and higher rates. It takes 1 to 3 working days, and you can keep working while it is reviewed.

Why do you need my home address?

We use it to calculate the distance between you and each customer, which decides which requests you receive and the transport fee. Customers only see the distance, never your address.

How is the transport fee calculated?

It is based on distance bands (for example 0–2 km free, 2–5 km ฿30, 5–10 km ฿60). It is paid to you in full. The final method will be confirmed later.

How much does MochiMaid charge?

MochiMaid takes a commission only on completed bookings. The exact rate is not finalised. The rate shown is 15% on the cleaning fee and add-ons.

Can I decline a request?

Yes. You choose which jobs to accept. Declining does not affect your rating, but cancelling after accepting affects your reliability score.
